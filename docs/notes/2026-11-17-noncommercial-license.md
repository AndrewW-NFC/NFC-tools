# Noncommercial licensing decision

Requested and explicitly approved for implementation and commit on November 17,
2026, as an exception to the project hold.

I selected the unmodified PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0, with a separate
commercial licensing route for uses outside its permissions. Funded research
and conservation work may remain free under the standard license's permissions,
including its express organizational permissions regardless of funding.

The root LICENSE exactly matches the official standard text. NOTICE records my
copyright, commercial contact, and third-party and historical-release boundaries.
Package metadata and all three main README guides reflect the change. The draft
text is retained as a reference copy, not an additional license.

Earlier MIT releases and the historical source snapshot retain their original
permissions. Commercial agreements require a review of the rights I can grant,
including contribution rights and separate analyzer, model, and asset terms.
